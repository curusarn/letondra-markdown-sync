Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

What the app accesses

With your permission, Letondra Markdown Sync accesses Google Drive files in the folders you choose to sync. It reads Google Docs and their revision history to export them as Markdown, and it creates and updates Google Docs in those folders when you add or change notes locally.

Where data goes

Your files and your Google sign-in token stay on your own computer and in your own Google Drive. The app has no server and sends nothing to the developer or to any third party. It does not collect analytics, does not sell or share data, and does not use data for advertising.

Google API Services

Letondra Markdown Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete its local data by uninstalling it.
